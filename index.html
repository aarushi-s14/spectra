Pipeline

Physics, Not Vibes.

Every generative model must upscale its output. Upscaling is periodic by construction, and periodicity is exactly what a Fourier transform sees.

1

Patch & Filter

Each frame is cut into 32×32 patches and pushed through a Butterworth high-pass, stripping natural image structure and isolating fine texture.

2

Spectral Fingerprint

A 2D FFT turns every patch into a frequency fingerprint. Patches are averaged, and the radial energy profile is measured.

3

Learned Verdict

Grid-peak prominence, axis spikes, and band ratios feed a calibrated classifier trained on BigGAN, Stable Diffusion, and CelebDF face-swaps.

See What the Model Sees.

On the right: a real upscaler fingerprint: sharp spikes at rational frequencies, on-axis, periodic. Natural photos decay smoothly like 1/f². That asymmetry is the entire signal, and it survives JPEG compression, resizing, and re-encoding.

Because detection is pure signal processing, it runs at ~3 ms per frame on a laptop CPU, fast enough to scan live video call-by-call.
